# CraftBridge compatibility

CraftBridge compatibility is evidence-based and scoped to the tested engine, ECU, CraftBridge variant, and firmware version. This list does not claim general Mercury compatibility.

Community reports are submitted through [Test your Mercury / Add your ECU](https://github.com/OldButTalented/CraftBridge/issues/new?template=test-your-mercury.yml). A report does not change this list automatically. Entries are added only after maintainer review and approval.

## Status definitions

- **Maintainer verified** — physically tested and accepted by the CraftBridge maintainer.
- **Community verified** — a community test report with sufficient evidence has been reviewed and approved by the maintainer; it is not necessarily reproduced by the maintainer.
- **Reviewed negative result** — a reviewed test report documents that the stated CraftBridge variant/version did not work as expected on the identified engine/ECU configuration.
